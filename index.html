About Me

I am a Pre-Business student at Brigham Young University (expected graduation 2028) with experience in financial coaching and client service. As a Relationship Manager at Infinity Finance, I managed a portfolio of 120 clients, helping families review household budgets and stay on track with mortgage repayment goals — including four months working with clients in Australia ahead of the company’s US launch. Before that I led a weekly pool-service route of about 100 pools in Utah, diagnosing equipment problems and keeping customers happy.

This website was built for my Intro to Information Systems final project. It pulls together a professional résumé, an interactive Tableau dashboard built from LinkedIn job-posting data, a small JavaScript web app I wrote, and a hand-coded page about one of my favorite hobbies: wakesurfing.
