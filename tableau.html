Tableau Dashboard: LinkedIn Job Postings

For my Chapter 19 data visualization project, I built a Tableau workbook of eight charts analyzing a dataset of 6,328 LinkedIn job postings (2021–2023), joined with company information and employee benefit packages. The workbook explores questions like:

  • Which job domains pay the most on average? (Product Management tops the list.)
  • How does pay vary by industry and by whether a job offers childcare and a 401(k)?
  • Where are entry-level jobs concentrated geographically, in the US and worldwide?
  • Do companies with more LinkedIn followers post more jobs?
  • How have posting volume, remote work, and hiring trended over time — and what does a forecast say comes next?

The dashboard below is fully interactive: hover over marks for exact values, use the sheet tabs at the bottom of the visualization to switch between the eight charts (Prompt 1 through Prompt 8), and use the toolbar to download or view it full-screen. It is embedded live from my Tableau Public profile — not a screenshot.
